# F08 The harness plays every completed T04 week from its start state to the main menu; 9 of 15 meet their DOSBox-X record's screens in order, and the other 6 differ only in the record's polled reads of the match board

- **Status**: proposed
- **Task**: T08 (#40)
- **Edition**: 96.
- **Sources**: `runtime/emul/corpus.py`, `runtime/emul/match.py` (code `8c11c1f`, unicorn 2.1.4, the
  settings of F06); run records `runs/T08/T08-r0001`…`T08-r0015`; traces `traces/T08/*-week-r0001.jsonl`;
  the index `traces/T08/index.json` (derived from those, regenerated by
  `python3 runtime/emul/corpus.py --index-only`); the DOSBox-X week records `runs/T04/T04-week-r*.json`
  (F04) and their end savestates (`.cache/states/T04/`, the owner's Drive `states/T04/`).

## Answer

1. `[confirmed]` **The corpus.** T04 has 18 week records; 15 have status `completed` (`r0001`–`r0004`,
   `r0006`–`r0010`, `r0012`–`r0017`; `r0005`, `r0011` and `r0018` are aborted and out of scope). For
   each, the savestate whose sha256 is the record's `start_state.sha256` was found under
   `.cache/states/T04/` (the load record's end savestate for a session's first week, the previous
   week's end savestate otherwise) before any run. T04 continued its DOSBox-X session from that point
   (its records say "continued, not restored"); the harness restores the same state from the file with
   that sha256 (each T08 record's `start_state.sha256`). One run per week, `T08-r0001`…`r0015`, each
   with `--compare` against its T04 record; the index lists all 15 with the trace, its sha256, the
   run, the verdict, the result and the draws.
2. `[confirmed]` **Every run completed.** All 15 runs reached the main menu after the week (end status
   `completed`, no unsupported service): no week stopped at an unknown screen. The screens they
   answered include `T04-week-r0010`'s and `r0016`'s five PLAYER CONTRACT screens (Return each) and
   `r0017`'s (Y/N) question (N), the keys weeks.py recorded for those weeks (`actions.keys`). No
   completed T04 week reached a league match's ineligible-player stop or a sale.
3. `[confirmed]` **9 weeks: all screens met in order.** `T04-week-r0001`–`r0004` and `r0006`–`r0010`
   (`T08-r0001`–`r0009`): every screen of the record (7, or 16 for `r0010`) occurs, chars and
   attributes byte-identical, among the trace's key waits in the record's order.
4. `[confirmed]` **6 weeks: the comparison fails** (`T08-r0010`–`r0015`, status `aborted: comparison
   failed (completed)`), all from the third session (`T04-load-r0003`), whose records were taken by a
   weeks.py that polls a match board (`commentary` on screen) until it stands still for 5 reads and
   then presses Space, recording three observations of it: its first read, its read before the Space
   ("unchanged for 5 reads") and its last read. The comparison matches each record screen to a later
   key wait than the one before, so three observations of one key wait cannot all be met. The failing
   lines (the index's `verdict`):
   - `r0012`–`r0016`: `screen 7 (match, unchanged for 5 reads) (<digest>) not met in order`: the half-time
     board's three reads have one digest, equal to the key wait the trace's `screen 2 (match, first
     read)` was matched to (key wait 4 in `r0012`, 2 in the others).
   - `r0017`: `screen 2 (match, first read) (45aa3078dbc50520) not met in order`: this first read
     differs from the same board's read before the Space six steps later (`d76e9082…`, which is the
     trace's key wait 2): weeks.py read the board while it was still changing, which is not a key wait,
     and the harness records only key waits.
5. `[confirmed]` **Those 6 with the polled first and last reads set aside** (the index's
   `key_wait_reading`, a second reading that leaves the verdict as it is): every other screen of each
   record, including the read before each Space, occurs among the trace's key waits in the record's
   order. In `r0012`–`r0016` every set-aside read equals its board's read before the Space; in `r0017`
   all but the first read do.
6. `[confirmed]` **The draws.** In all 15 traces every draw satisfies F02 item 4's step
   (`seed' = seed*0x7ABD + 0x1B0F`, `value = ((seed'*n) >> 16) + 1`) and each `seed_before` is the
   previous `seed_after` (no break). The first draw's seed is 59, 60, 61, 62, 63, 64 in the six distinct
   weeks in session order (`r0001`–`r0004`, `r0010`, `r0017` and their repeats). The managed club's match runs from
   draw 0 to the `match return` mark (5,853; 5,478; 5,556; 5,594; 5,983; 6,216 draws).
7. `[confirmed]` **The three sessions play the same weeks.** The traces from `T04-week-r0001`, `r0006`
   and `r0012`'s start states (three different savestates) have identical draws, results, end
   `state_digests` and `files_written`; so do `r0002`/`r0007`/`r0013`, `r0003`/`r0008`/`r0014`,
   `r0004`/`r0009`/`r0015` and `r0010`/`r0016`. The corpus is 15 traces of 6 distinct weeks: results
   BLACKBURN R 0–2 P ST GERMAIN (23,006 draws), SEVILLA 1–2 BLACKBURN R (22,554), CELTIC 2–2 BLACKBURN
   R (22,643), BLACKBURN R 2–0 ZAGLEBIE LUBIN (22,695), BLACKBURN R 1–1 NOTTINGHAM F (21,574),
   SHEFFIELD WED 1–3 BLACKBURN R (21,606). `[derived]` The DOSBox-X savestates differ but the game
   state each week starts from does not, as far as these traces measure it (draws, screens, the
   save-block digests at the end).
