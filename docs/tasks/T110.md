- **Evidence**: [confirmed:T103, runs/T103/table-r0001.json] the table block's
  layout (one 12-byte row per club, `P | PTS | W-D-L home/away | F u16 | A u16`)
  proved by the round, and the 2/1/0 points rule; the receipt claims no ordering.
  [confirmed:T105/T103, runs/T105/seed-r0001.json] the displayed positions of all
