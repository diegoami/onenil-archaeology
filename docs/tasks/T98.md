# T98 Remove the numeric audit's hooks when its caller returns

- **Kind**: fix · **Issue**: #214 · **Branch**: `task/T98-audit-hooks`.
- **Evidence**: [confirmed:#214][confirmed:T93, runs/T93/aborted-r0002 and
  sevilla-week-r0002] `NumericAudit.enter` adds two read hooks over all memory
  except the caller's stack window plus a write hook, and nothing removes them.
  On the same Unicorn 2.1.4 instance, later guest code that reaches the live
  stack through those hooks dies with `UC_ERR_INSN_INVALID at 2864:B941`; a
  restored machine without hooks reaches the management menu, and no-op read
  hooks alone reproduce the fault on the sixth key. T93 mitigated this with
  `fresh_instance`, which discards emulator-side state.
- **Owns**: `runtime/emul/postmatch_tail.py` hook lifetime, `runtime/emul/sevilla_week.py`,
  `runtime/emul/sevilla_match.py`, `runtime/emul/fresh_postmatch.py` driver
  sequencing, `runs/T98/**`, `tests/emul/test_audit_hooks.py`, documentation.
- **Scope**: [designed] keep the three hook handles, and delete them once the
  caller's `Complete` has propagated out of `emu_start` - never inside a hook
  callback, where the fault is raised. The drivers must close the audit after
  their play segment and may then continue on the same instance; the T93
  `fresh_instance` step is removed once the same machine reproduces the
  committed week facts. Records, counts and the caller/condition expectations
  do not change; the audit still records exactly what it recorded before.
- **Milestones**: frozen task; two-phase probe and fail-before record; hook
  lifetime fix and driver sequencing; same-instance week check; suites and
  exact-head CI; merge.
- **Done when**:
  1. `python3 runs/T98/probe.py --phase hooks` reaches the documented emulator
     fault, and `--phase closed` completes the week route with week facts equal
     to `runs/T93/week-suffix-r0003`; both read the digest-pinned private
     checkpoint and write fresh files under `records/T98/`.
  2. `python3 -m pytest tests/emul/test_audit_hooks.py -q` fails before the task
     and then passes: registered handles are deleted, closing twice is safe, and
     each driver closes after its play segment.
  3. `python3 runtime/emul/sevilla_week.py --resume runs/T93/sevilla-week-r0002/checkpoint-caller.json --directory <new>`
     still reproduces the committed week facts, and full CI passes.
- **Hazards**: deleting a hook inside its own callback re-enters Unicorn and
  crashes; close only after `play` returns. Do not weaken the audit's exclusions
  or skip its write hook. No capture concurrency; one Rust build at a time is
  irrelevant here, but no original capture may run beside the probe.
